at commit 99c678e569c7c4c0772db297c5ddd5e4c9b6322e, under Apache-2.0. The full
license is in FORMALIZATION-STANDARD-LICENSE.txt.

The taxonomy JSON files are unmodified copies from PalomarSubmission at commit
65f0154ed776cd26c224254aa57b379137f28b0d. Original attribution and licensing are
preserved in PALOMAR-TAXONOMIES-LICENSE.md. MSC2020 data is derived from
Mathematical Reviews and zbMATH and distributed under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. The
arXiv data derives from the public arXiv category taxonomy. These third-party
data files are not relicensed by the package GPL notice.

source-pins.json records exact immutable URLs and SHA-256 digests.
PALOMAR-SUBMISSION-LICENSE.txt preserves the upstream software MIT license,
which upstream distinguishes from its third-party data licenses.
